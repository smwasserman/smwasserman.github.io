Silly Samuel's Silly Store

An online store for AI air fryers and DVDs of Norbit, designed to be as hard to use as possible. It breaks twelve rules of good interface design on purpose: runaway login buttons, a "Remove" button that adds items, 99,451 zip codes in random order, and a checkout that charges you a mandatory tip.

You can still log in (username name, password 12345), shop, and check out. It just fights you the whole way.

Warning: the store has flashing, spinning and shaking content. You'll be asked about animations before it loads, and you can switch them off from any page.

What it gets wrong, and why

Every store page has an Explain the bad UX switch in the bottom-left corner. Turn it on to see each broken rule marked on the page, with a note on what it breaks and how.
